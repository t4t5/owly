02 / RADIO PHYSICS IS RUDE

Wi-Fi stays up.
Conditions apply.

Shared mode leaves wlan0 connected while Owly uses a monitor interface. Your access point must be on AWDL social channel 6, 44, or 149. One radio cannot occupy two channels at once.

This is an experimental build. The Nexmon Pi 5 port is still under upstream review, and two-way AirDrop plus simultaneous Wi-Fi have not yet been verified on physical Pi 5 or CM5 hardware. Testers are welcome.
